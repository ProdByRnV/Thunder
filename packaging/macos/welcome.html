This installs Thunder as an AU, VST3 and CLAP plugin for all users of this Mac. To leave a format out, click Customize on the Installation Type step.

Thunder runs on macOS 11 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

When it's installed, open your DAW and rescan its plugins, then add Thunder as an effect on the track with your melody. If Logic Pro or GarageBand doesn't list Thunder, restart your Mac.

Your presets and captures are saved in Documents/Thunder.
