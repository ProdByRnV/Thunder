Installing Thunder on macOS
===========================

Thunder runs on macOS 11 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, in any DAW
that loads AU, VST3 or CLAP plugins.

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders. In Finder, choose Go > Go to
   Folder... and enter each folder below. Create a folder if it isn't there.

   AU (Logic Pro, GarageBand):  Thunder.component
                                ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

   VST3:                        Thunder.vst3
                                ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

   CLAP:                        Thunder.clap
                                ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP

2. Allow Thunder to open. macOS holds back plugins downloaded from the internet
   until you allow them. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities), paste this
   line and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Thunder.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Thunder.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Thunder.clap

   It changes nothing but these three plugins. If you left one of them out,
   Terminal says it can't find it; you can ignore that.

   If your DAW still says Thunder can't be opened, open System Settings >
   Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the message
   about Thunder.

3. Open your DAW and rescan its plugins. If Logic Pro or GarageBand doesn't
   list Thunder, restart your Mac.

4. Add Thunder as an effect on the track with your melody.

Your presets and captures are saved in Documents/Thunder.

To uninstall, delete the three plugins from those folders.
