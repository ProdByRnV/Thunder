Installing Thunder on Linux
===========================

Thunder runs on 64-bit Linux with glibc 2.35 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12,
Fedora 36 or newer), on the processor this download is for (x86_64 or ARM64),
in any DAW that loads VST3, CLAP or LV2 plugins, such as REAPER, Bitwig Studio
or Ardour. It uses FreeType and Fontconfig, which desktop systems already have.

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders:

   VST3:  Thunder.vst3 (inside "VST3")  ->  ~/.vst3
   CLAP:  Thunder.clap (inside "CLAP")  ->  ~/.clap
   LV2:   Thunder.lv2 (inside "LV2")    ->  ~/.lv2

   Or open a terminal in this folder and run:

     mkdir -p ~/.vst3 ~/.clap ~/.lv2
     cp -r VST3/Thunder.vst3 ~/.vst3/
     cp CLAP/Thunder.clap ~/.clap/
     cp -r LV2/Thunder.lv2 ~/.lv2/

   You only need the formats your DAW uses.

2. Open your DAW and rescan its plugins.

3. Add Thunder as an effect on the track with your melody.

Your presets and captures are saved in Thunder, in your Documents folder
(usually ~/Documents/Thunder).

To uninstall, delete ~/.vst3/Thunder.vst3, ~/.clap/Thunder.clap and
~/.lv2/Thunder.lv2.
