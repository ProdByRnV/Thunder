Installing Thunder on Windows
=============================

Thunder runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), in any DAW that loads VST3 or CLAP
plugins.

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders:

   VST3:  the "Thunder.vst3" folder (inside "VST3")
          ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   CLAP:  "Thunder.clap" (inside "CLAP")
          ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP

   Create the CLAP folder if it isn't there. Windows asks for administrator
   permission to copy into these folders.

2. Open your DAW and rescan its plugins. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins
   > Find installed plugins.

3. Add Thunder as an effect on the track with your melody.

Your presets and captures are saved in Documents\Thunder.

To uninstall, delete Thunder.vst3 and Thunder.clap from those folders.
